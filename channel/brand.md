## Vaat (kanal açıklaması, EN)
"Every episode, a real mystery solved by a chart — rebuilt from the original numbers. Plotted Past retells the moments when data changed what people believed: epidemics traced on maps, disasters hidden in scatterplots, polls that fooled a nation. All visuals are generated from cited data; narration is a synthetic voice (Kokoro). Sources in every description."
